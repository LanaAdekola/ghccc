# Search Console setup

The owner adds the domain verification TXT record supplied by Google in Namecheap Advanced DNS, then clicks Verify in the church-owned Search Console domain property. A TXT record alone does not prove account verification.

After canonical URLs are corrected and production redeployed, submit https://www.ghccglobal.com/sitemap.xml. Inspect the homepage and one published sermon/event URL. Invite the media pastor as a Full user; church representatives retain ownership. Search Console verification is not an advertising tag.
